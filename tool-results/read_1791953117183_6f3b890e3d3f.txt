     1→# Airbnb — Complete Features & Functions Master Specification
     2→
     3→This expanded specification covers Airbnb's major marketplace capabilities and the additional features required to build a complete, production-ready Airbnb-style platform for Stayora.
     4→
     5→It goes beyond a list of website pages. It defines what each module should do, the actions users can perform, the business rules behind those actions, the admin controls, and the systems that connect everything.
     6→
     7→The Stayora design must remain consistent with your existing direction: navy blue, white, peach, and orange; left sidebar navigation; clean typography; rounded cards; soft shadows; and a simple, modern interface. The goal is to expand functionality without redesigning your existing pages.

   349→## 6. Booking engine and reservation management
   350→
   351→This module requires the strongest validation because it controls inventory and financial transactions.
   352→
   353→### 6.1 Availability rules
   354→
   355→- Check-in must precede check-out.
   356→- Enforce maximum occupancy.
   357→- Enforce minimum and maximum stay duration.
   358→- Prevent reservations on blocked dates.
   359→- Prevent overlapping confirmed reservations.
   360→- Support preparation buffers between bookings.
   361→- Enforce advance-booking windows.
   362→- Support minimum lead time.
   363→- Support check-in and check-out restrictions.
   364→- Support seasonal availability.
   365→- Account for local dates and time zones.
   366→- Allow authorized hosts to block personal dates.
   367→- Revalidate availability during checkout and before confirmation.
   368→
   369→### 6.2 Reservation creation
   370→
   371→1. Receive selected property, dates, guests, and guest identity.
   372→2. Recalculate availability and price on the server.
   373→3. Create a temporary inventory hold where the chosen booking model supports it.
   374→4. Create the booking with a unique reference.
   375→5. Initiate payment or host approval as required.
   376→6. Process the payment provider's authoritative result.
   377→7. Confirm the reservation atomically with inventory updates.
   378→8. Send confirmation notifications.
   379→9. Update the guest dashboard, host dashboard, and calendar.
   380→10. Record the transaction and audit events.
   381→
   382→A temporary hold must expire safely. A payment failure must not permanently block inventory, and retrying a request must not create duplicate reservations.
   383→

   417→## 7. Pricing, discounts, fees, and taxes
   418→
   419→A marketplace needs a dedicated pricing engine rather than a single price field on each listing.
   420→
   421→### Host pricing controls
   422→
   423→- Base nightly rate.
   424→- Weekend rates.
   425→- Seasonal rates.
   426→- Date-specific overrides.
   427→- Weekly and monthly discounts.
   428→- Early-booking discounts.
   429→- Last-minute discounts.
   430→- Promotional rates.
   431→- Minimum and maximum stay.
   432→- Cleaning fee.
   433→- Extra-guest fee where permitted.
   434→- Security-deposit or damage-policy handling where legally and operationally supported.
   435→- Currency selection subject to supported markets.
   436→
   437→### Pricing calculation
   438→
   439→A recommended model:
   440→
   441→\\[ \text{Accommodation Subtotal} =\sum\_{d=\text{check-in}}^{\text{check-out}-1} \text{Nightly Rate}(d) \\]
   442→
   443→\\[ \text{Pre-Tax Total} =\text{Subtotal}-\text{Discounts}+\text{Fees} \\]
   444→
   445→\\[ \text{Final Total} =\text{Pre-Tax Total}+\text{Applicable Taxes} \\]
   446→
   447→This is a conceptual formula. The actual calculation order, fee basis, tax treatment, rounding, and currency conversion rules must be explicitly defined for each supported market.
   448→
   449→### Pricing functions
   450→
   451→- Calculate a quote before checkout.
   452→- Return an itemized breakdown.
   453→- Recalculate quotes when dates or guest counts change.
   454→- Validate coupon eligibility and expiry.
   455→- Prevent incompatible discounts from being stacked.
   456→- Store the quote and price components associated with a confirmed booking.
   457→- Record currency and rounding rules.
   458→- Display the total cost transparently.
   459→- Generate receipts and transaction reports.
   460→- Reconcile booking amounts with payment records.

  1093→## 19. Backend API specification
  1094→
  1095→The following is a suggested API contract for Stayora. These are proposed endpoints, not Airbnb's private APIs.
  1096→
  1097→| API group       | Example endpoints                                        | Purpose                                |
  1098→| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
  1099→| Authentication  | `POST /api/auth/register`, `POST /api/auth/login`        | Identity and sessions                  |
  1100→| Search          | `GET /api/search`, `GET /api/destinations`               | Search and discovery                   |
  1101→| Listings        | `GET /api/listings/:id`, `POST /api/listings`            | Public listing and host CRUD           |
  1102→| Availability    | `GET /api/listings/:id/availability`                     | Calendar and available inventory       |
  1103→| Quotes          | `POST /api/quotes`                                       | Calculate itemized price               |
  1104→| Bookings        | `POST /api/bookings`, `GET /api/bookings/:id`            | Create and retrieve reservations       |
  1105→| Booking changes | `POST /api/bookings/:id/change-requests`                 | Modify reservations                    |
  1106→| Cancellations   | `POST /api/bookings/:id/cancellations`                   | Cancel under applicable policy         |
  1107→| Payments        | `POST /api/payments`, `POST /api/webhooks/payments`      | Payment initiation and provider events |
  1108→| Payouts         | `GET /api/host/payouts`                                  | Host payout history                    |
  1109→| Messages        | `GET /api/conversations`, `POST /api/messages`           | Guest-host messaging                   |
  1110→| Wishlists       | `GET /api/wishlists`, `POST /api/wishlists`              | Saved properties                       |
  1111→| Reviews         | `POST /api/reviews`, `GET /api/listings/:id/reviews`     | Review submission and retrieval        |
  1112→| Notifications   | `GET /api/notifications`, `PATCH /api/notifications/:id` | Notification center                    |
  1113→| Support         | `POST /api/support/tickets`                              | Customer support                       |
  1114→| Admin           | `/api/admin/*`                                           | Protected operations and moderation    |
  1115→| Analytics       | `/api/analytics/*`                                       | Approved dashboards and reports        |
  1116→
  1117→### API requirements
  1118→
  1119→- Authenticate protected endpoints.
  1120→- Authorize resource ownership and role permissions.
  1121→- Validate every request on the server.
  1122→- Apply pagination to collection endpoints.
  1123→- Use consistent error responses.
  1124→- Rate-limit sensitive actions.
  1125→- Use idempotency keys for critical payment and booking operations.
  1126→- Validate webhook signatures.
  1127→- Log important state changes without exposing secrets.
  1128→- Avoid trusting prices, permissions, or payment success supplied by the client.
